More: emergency signs and what the colors do not mean

The maps and colors are game-only exposure indicators. They cannot diagnose, exclude, locate, or grade a real injury. After head impact, worsening headache, repeated vomiting, seizure, or declining alertness are emergency warning signs: call local emergency services (119 in South Korea).
